## Standing constraints worth not re-learning

`npx tsc --noEmit` **passes silently** — it resolves the root solution config and checks nothing.
Use `npm run build`. `dims.json` is read at boot by every page and is capped at 2.89 MB; per-species
payload belongs in `profiles.json`. **The dump contains no egg, clutch, hatch or fertility record**
— asked for three times now; `core/metrics.ts`'s `UNSOURCED` registry declares it and
`core/checks.ts` asserts against it at boot. Scores travel as `[value, outOf]` because welfare is
1–5 while `budget_score` is 0–20.

## Deliberately not built, with the reason

The Eggs tab carries reproductive biology and recorded young, not the requested laid/fertile/
discarded figures — there is no source. "Ready to breed" is rendered as "Both sexes present"
because maturity needs an age and `born` is unusable on 81% of the register. `PAIRS` is absent
because no pairing record exists and two reasonable derivations disagree by 4×.
