Both summed to 110,020, so neither looked wrong alone. The table is deleted; every surface now reads `sexSplit` in `core/animals.ts`, whose own comment had already predicted this ("would invent an unsexed share of about 4%"). **Do not reintroduce a rate table.** (2) `band()` in `modules/regulatory.ts` used `rows.length` for its species count, but `holdings()` is keyed per `<site>:<name>` registry pair — so one page read "2,411 Species" in its hero and 4,745 two sections down.
