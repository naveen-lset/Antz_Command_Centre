Hero foliage, and the image that does not stop

Two fixes, both in every study. One — the vines, monstera and wisteria at the left and right of the artwork were being greyed out by the banner gradient washed across the top of the picture, which is exactly where that foliage lives. The gradient is gone and the top of the artwork is no longer faded at all, so the foliage is at full strength. Two — the picture no longer ends above Executive KPIs; it carries on behind the cards.

The artwork is never zoomed past 100% of the column, because the foliage sits at its extreme left and right and any crop takes off the thing we just made visible. So the hero is 330px and reaching the cards is a second, washed layer of the same scene. The five differ in how far that continuation runs and how strong it is — and 5 keeps it out of the cards' way entirely.

1 · Short continuation
Foliage full strength. The scene carries just past the Executive KPI heading, then hands over to the shade. The lightest touch that still answers "don't stop before the cards".
2 · Behind the KPI block
The scene runs behind the whole Executive KPI grid before dissolving. The cards float on the park rather than starting after it.
3 · Through to Trends
Continues past the KPIs and into Trends, thinning the whole way. The furthest the picture can run before it starts reading as a second image rather than the same one.
4 · Whole page, very light
The scene never ends — it just keeps thinning to the foot of the page at about a tenth strength. The most continuous, and the least present at any one point.
5 · Edges only below the hero
The continuation is cut to the two margins, so it runs down the sides at real strength and the channel the white cards sit in stays clean. Continuing the image costs the content nothing.
