Home background — five studies

Same page, same hero, same cards in all five. No drawn scenery anywhere — the vector trees, bushes, ponds and fences are gone; the only illustration on the page is the one photograph at the top. What differs between the columns is the ground the whole scroll is painted on, and nothing else.

All five hold #d6e8dd — the canopy colour, the shade the artwork's own fade lands on — from the top of the page down to the foot of the illustration. That stop is fixed: any other colour there puts a visible floor under the elephants. The five are five answers to what the shade does after that.

1 · Flat hold
One colour, top to bottom. No gradient below the hero at all — literally the same shade until the end.
2 · Long descent
One ramp stretched over the whole page, canopy to a lighter green. Never reaches the old flat sage, so it never runs out.
3 · Envelope
Deeper at both ends, lightest through the middle where the cards are densest. The page closes on the colour it opened in.
4 · Atmospheric hold
Flat canopy with three enormous soft pools of light drifting down it. No shape and no edge — only luminosity.
5 · Haze steps
The shade lightens about one level per section band, the way distance does, with the steps dissolved so depth never becomes a line.
