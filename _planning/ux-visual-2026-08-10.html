Legibility and touch

Two fixes that cost almost nothing and apply to all twenty-two module pages at once.

TodayThe most-used caption colour fails AA
1,284 Dispensed · BAND 8.2–9.8 · 11 measures
#9b958b on white — 2.97:1

AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 even for large text. This colour carries every unit, caption, section aside and sub-label in the product, usually at 11–12 px. On the sage ground it falls to 2.55:1.

Scope pills are 33 px tall, horizon chips 28 px, chevrons 14×14. The dashed ring is the 44 px touch floor. 25 targets miss it on a phone.

The token already exists. MUTED is in the palette, looks all but identical, and passes. FAINT stays — for hairlines and de-emphasised marks, where contrast rules do not apply.

Grown through padding, not visual size — the pill can look the same and still be hittable. Scope is the control the whole product hangs on.
