## Session 2 — Lab Details Page

**Lab** (`v4/modules/lab.tsx` rewritten, plus new `labData.ts` and `labSheets.tsx`) replaced the
last hand-authored module on the report track. Every figure now derives from one walk of the
`lab` daily series: `labData.ts` adds the LIMS view core lacks — request status, turnaround,
positive/negative, flagged — seeded off `rng(ev.id)` so a tally and the records behind it are
the same draw made twice. Departments and their SLAs are the real `LAB_DEPARTMENTS` registry,
so TAT is measured against each bench's own `turnaround` rather than an authored target. The
reporting-lag parameters are calibrated so the derived pending queue equals `labOpen` = 31,
the figure Home publishes and `core/checks.ts` asserts — change them and the page contradicts
the KPI the director tapped to reach it. Nothing committed. **Eggs was NOT rebuilt in v4; it
is still the original 258-line module here, and a full Eggs page exists only in the 5200
worktree, built on that tree's incompatible `src/exec` architecture.**

### Defect found in shared code (worked around, not fixed)

`core/events.ts` draws a lab event's `detail` (sample type) and `labDeptId` (bench) as two
independent weighted draws, so a request could read "Serology at the Toxicology bench". Every
pre-existing breakdown reads one dimension or the other, so it never surfaced. `labData.ts`
now treats the department as authoritative and derives the assay from what that bench runs.
`core` itself is untouched — if the two dimensions should be linked at source, that is a
`core/events.ts` change and it would affect the `detail` tally on the Lab entity page.

navigates to the module's details page, and the sheet is the drill you open *from* that page.

## Session 3 — Eggs & Incubation

**Eggs** (`v4/modules/eggs.tsx` rewritten, plus new `eggsData.ts` and `eggsSheets.tsx`).
Unlike Lab, core already owned most of this model: `eggs`, `hatched` and `discarded` are three
real flow series asserted at 142 / 96 / 13 for the month in `core/checks.ts`, and the three
nurseries, thirteen incubators, species and vocabularies are all registry entries. So
`eggsData.ts` adds only the two things nothing models — whether a hatchling survived (seeded
per hatch) and which species hatched here for the first time (one scan of the whole ledger,
never over a window). The page reproduces 142 / 96 / 68% / 13 exactly.

### The decision that shapes the page

Laid and hatched are NOT treated as one cohort here, deliberately, and this is the opposite of
the sibling Eggs build in the 5200 worktree. Core states its own position in a comment on the
metric: `hatched` is "its own metric rather than a fraction of `eggs`, because a clutch laid in
July may hatch in August". Overriding that would put a second, disagreeing hatch count beside
the one `checks.ts` asserts. It is safe here because the authored series keep hatched +
discarded ≤ laid at every window and site — verified across all ten windows, hatch rate 60–80%.
What does invert is a single day, on 914 site-days out of 13,152. So **hatch percentage is only
ever computed at window grain**, and the trend draws set and hatched SIDE BY SIDE rather than
stacked — a stacked day column would draw a segment taller than its own total.

### User-visible acceptance

1. **Hero** — `#/eggs` reads 142 eggs set, 96 hatched, 68% hatch, 13 discarded, 93 survived,
   3 mortality. *If any of the first four differ, the page has stopped agreeing with
   `core/checks.ts`.*
2. **Nursery split sums** — Avian 96 / Reptile 34 / Aquatic 12 = 142; hatched 62 / 24 / 10 = 96.
3. **Discard is never mortality** — "Egg outcome": Discarded hangs off Eggs set, Mortality hangs
   off Hatched. Two cards say so in words. *If discard ever appears under hatched, the
   lifecycle nesting broke.*
4. **Drill** — Nursery-wise table row → Incubators → Species → a record. Four levels, one sheet.
5. **Record ids are unique** — an egg and its hatching are `EGG-…` and `HAT-…`. *Duplicate-key
   warnings in the console mean the stage prefix was stripped again.*
6. **Survival reads ~97% on the month and ~91% all-time.** That is correct, not a bug: loss

7. **First hatch on record** — four species on record, none in July, so the card shows the most
   recent four under an explicit "none in this window" line rather than going blank.

## DEFERRED — agreed with Naveen 2026-08-10, to do later

Five gaps between the v4 Eggs page and the earlier 5200 build. All are known and accepted, not
bugs. Listed in the order I would do them.

- [ ] **1 · Eggs-laid calendar.** The only one that is a real loss of capability. A day heat
      grid over the `eggs` series alone, tap a date → that day's clutches. Build it against
      `daily('eggs', siteKey)` in `eggsData.ts` and add a `laidDay` sheet state to
      `eggsSheets.tsx`. **Constraint: it must plot ONE series.** Do not put hatched on the same
      calendar and do not derive a per-day hatch rate from it — the two series are on different
      clocks and invert on 914 site-days out of 13,152.

- [ ] **2 · Discard by species and by site.** `eggs.tsx` "Egg discard" currently offers reasons
      and by-nursery. Add a dimension toggle like the one in "Hatch percentage". Species groups
      already exist on the cut (`base.species`); site is the global header filter, so a by-site
      row only makes sense with the header set to All sites.

- [ ] **3 · Scientific names.** Species carry only a common name. Needs a binomial on the
      `Species` record in `core/world.ts` — a core change, so it touches every module that
      names a species, not just Eggs. Decide whether that is wanted before starting.

- [ ] **4 · Separate site-wise table.** Only worth doing if a site ever runs more than one
      nursery. Today `NURSERIES` is one per site, so the nursery table already is the site
      table and a second one would print the same three rows under a different heading.

- [ ] **5 · "Incubating" as a fourth state.** Not modelled in v4: `eggs`, `hatched` and
      `discarded` are three independent flows with no in-progress egg. Adding it means either a
      fourth metric in `core/metrics.ts` or switching Eggs to a laid-cohort model — and the
      cohort model is what `core`'s own comment on `hatched` argues against. Largest of the
      five and the one most likely not to be worth it.

Also still open from earlier sessions: nothing is committed anywhere in this worktree, and the
`core/events.ts` independent-draw defect (test vs department) is worked around in `labData.ts`
rather than fixed at source.
