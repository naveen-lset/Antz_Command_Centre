## Two judgment calls worth knowing before you change them

1. **The population axis is fitted, not zero-based.** A 215k headcount moving 1.2k on a zero-based
   axis is a flat line, which is technically honest and useless. The band is padded by a third of
   the data's own range at each end and **both ends are printed inside the plot** — a fitted axis
   with its floor stated cannot be mistaken for a zero-based one. Counts (`EventTrend`) do start at
   zero. Don't remove those two figures; they are what makes the fit honest.
2. **The mortality series wears the accent, not red.** Severity is carried by the "highest impact"
   band, the delta and the peak marker. This follows the brief's "status colours are not chart
   series colours" — if you paint the columns red, half the page becomes an alarm.

   Births and Mortality show the same against the previous month. *If a comparison is missing, the

   month grid with day numbers and counts, 45 births total. Switch the window to Yesterday: the
   calendar disappears (under 7 days) and the trend collapses to a single figure with `+1 vs 29 Jul`.
   *A GitHub-style strip instead of a month grid means the window is over ~7 weeks — that's correct.*
5. **Regulatory reads as two percentages.** `#/animals` → Regulatory standing: `14%` / `86%` facing

7. **Escapes are incidents, not a bar chart.** `#/animals` → Escaped animals: a dated rail with

8. **Eggs is a funnel.** `#/eggs` → Egg outcome: Set → Hatched → Survived as centred tapering

   *of*. *If discard ever appears as a stage inside the funnel, the nesting broke — a discarded egg
   is not a dead hatchling.*
9. **Nothing regressed.** All 22 module pages render with zero console errors; `npx tsc -p
