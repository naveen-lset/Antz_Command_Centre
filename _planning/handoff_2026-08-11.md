## Two judgment calls worth knowing before you change them

1. **The population axis is fitted, not zero-based.** A 215k headcount moving 1.2k on a zero-based
   axis is a flat line, which is technically honest and useless. The band is padded by a third of
   the data's own range at each end and **both ends are printed inside the plot** — a fitted axis
   with its floor stated cannot be mistaken for a zero-based one. Counts (`EventTrend`) do start at
   zero. Don't remove those two figures; they are what makes the fit honest.
2. **The mortality series wears the accent, not red.** Severity is carried by the "highest impact"
   band, the delta and the peak marker. This follows the brief's "status colours are not chart
   series colours" — if you paint the columns red, half the page becomes an alarm.

## What each part is for

- **`MIST`** (`Home.tsx`) — the ground colour rising through the artwork's foot. The mask only
  makes the artwork thinner; a wash takes its *contrast* out first, which is what distance
  actually does. The two ramps are deliberately offset. Its radial's last stop is transparent —

- **`.env-home` / `.env-page`** (`index.css`) — two environment profiles. Module pages keep the

  the illustration gets a scene's distant *treeline* instead of its near-field canopies. Without
  it, two full-size tree crowns showed between the KPI cards as grey lozenges.
- **`.page-ground` + `Ground`** (`Shell.tsx`) — one ground for the whole window. `--env-canopy`

## Three judgment calls worth knowing before you change them

1. **`--env-top` uses window media queries, not container queries.** It tracks a *column*
   measurement, so container queries were right — but the shell's ground has to be painted
   outside the column where no container query reaches. The two breakpoints (`520 / 545 / 652`
   at `<460 / 460 / 1216`) are `.tier`'s column steps translated through the rail arithmetic.
   **If `--rail` or `--shell-pad` is ever retuned, 1216 is the number that moves with it.**
2. **The shell's ground carries no z-index.** `-z-20` there escaped to the root stacking context
   and painted underneath the shell's own background — invisible. Making the shell isolate would
   fix it *and* trap the search overlay's `z-50`, putting an open sheet's `z-40` on top of a
   search the reader just opened. It relies on paint order instead: first child, with the column
   row after it made `relative`. **Don't remove that `relative`.**
3. **The fade is ~130–170px and not longer.** The elephants stand at about 78% of the image
   height; a deeper wash takes them with the grass. Going deeper needs a taller hero band, which
   is a layout change and was out of scope.

### Three decisions worth knowing before you change them

1. **Sorting runs over all fifty and then pages.** `rows` is sorted whole and `page.rows` is a
   slice of it, so "which site has the most overdue" opens on the worst site, not the worst of
   the printed eight. Page the rows before sorting them and the table still looks right while
   answering a different question. The dep passed to `usePaged` is `rows` itself, **not
   `rows.length`** — a scope change recuts all fifty values without changing how many there
   are, and a length dep would serve stale rows.
2. **`TileGrid` is given `min={104}`, not the 132px default.** That card sits in a half-width
   column, where 132px fits three across — seventeen rows, a wall rather than small multiples.
   A narrower tile hides nothing, so it is the lever to reach for before capping a list.
3. **The supplement site split renders only when something was administered.**
   `medical_records` stops 25 Apr 2026 against a 20 May 2026 clock, so on the default window
   every site has given nothing. The flow is real — 3,929 administrations across the extract —
   but fifty tiles each reading 0 assert a comparison fifty times that `EventTrend` above has
   already denied once in a sentence.

### A rotation that was a ranking

`RotationCycle` printed **"Cycle position 3 of 4"** under every anthelmintic, which reads as
where that drug sits in the worming cycle. Its only caller passes `byDimension(scope,
'deworming', 'detail')`, and `core/events.ts` sorts a tally **by value descending** — so the
stated cycle position was the drug's rank by volume, relabelled. The schema has no sequence,
cycle or round column to derive a real one from. With **sixty distinct anthelmintics** it also
read "Cycle position 47 of 60".

confident stated one that is wrong. The track survives — a drug's share of the window is real,
and a lean on one drug is the question worth asking. The claim under it is gone, rows are one
line instead of two, and the list names eight and counts the tail (`52 more · 5,746 treatments
· 37% of the window`) rather than printing sixty. Every drug is still in the track. 2,152px to
424px. The card's `Rule` is now "By anthelmintic".

### One that looked like a bug and was not

The Deworming card reads `Dewormed 0 of 110,020` when measured from a headless browser. It is

believing a figure read over CDP** — the same trap applies to any screenshot of a long page.
